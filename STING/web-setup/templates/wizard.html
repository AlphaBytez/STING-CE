macOS Users: Keep Your Terminal Handy!

Installation takes 30-60 minutes (longer on VMs or slower hardware). macOS sudo credentials expire after ~5 minutes of inactivity.

If installation pauses:

  1. Go to your terminal window (where you ran the installer)
  2. Run: sudo -v
  3. Enter your password or use TouchID
  4. Installation will automatically resume

💡 Pro tip: Keep your terminal visible during installation. You may see a password prompt there.
